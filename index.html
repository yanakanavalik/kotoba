Kotoba

A quiet place to collect Japanese words — for iPhone and iPad.

Support

Found a bug, a wrong word, or have an idea? Please open an issue on GitHub. Include your device and iOS version if something isn't working.

Common questions

No voice when I tap a word? Kotoba uses your device's built-in Japanese voice. Check that your ringer/silent switch and volume are on. You can add higher-quality voices in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Japanese.

Where is my progress saved? Only on your device. Deleting the app also deletes it.

Privacy

Kotoba collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
